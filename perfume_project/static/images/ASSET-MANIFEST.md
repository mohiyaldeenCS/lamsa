# LAMSA visual assets

The six images in this directory are original AI-generated illustrations for the fictional LAMSA catalog. They are not photographs of physical inventory or third-party branded products. Product photos are intentionally reused by fragrance family.

| Asset | Art direction |
| --- | --- |
| `hero-campaign.png` | Green glass perfume bottle, amber liquid, travertine, foliage, and warm natural light. |
| `perfume-rose.png` | Blush glass bottle and rose petals in soft studio light. |
| `perfume-ocean.png` | Blue glass bottle with cool aquatic lighting and pale stones. |
| `perfume-musk.png` | Smoky amber glass, a dark cap, wood, and warm neutral tones. |
| `perfume-vanilla.png` | Golden glass and vanilla ingredients in a cream-colored scene. |
| `perfume-citrus.png` | Pistachio-green glass with citrus ingredients and fresh lighting. |

All images use a consistent LAMSA label, realistic glass, and restrained studio compositions. No external image hosts or runtime image services are required.

The Tajawal font is distributed separately under its original SIL Open Font License in `../fonts/OFL.txt`. Required third-party notices must remain intact.
